# RMC Care Connect

# MASTER PROMPT — ROYAL MEDICAL CENTER DIGITAL FRONT DOOR

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build a highly polished, production-quality FRONT-END DEMO of a next-generation digital healthcare platform for:

**Royal Medical Center — Doha, Qatar**

Reference website and branding source:

https://rmcdoha.com/

The product should be positioned as:

# "The Digital Front Door of Royal Medical Center"

This is NOT simply a redesign of the existing RMC website.

It is a modern digital patient experience platform that sits on top of the medical center's existing services and transforms how patients:

* Discover the right medical service
* Find the appropriate doctor
* Understand where to go
* Request/book appointments
* Describe their symptoms
* Prepare for appointments
* Receive follow-up information
* Navigate the medical center digitally

The demo must feel like a serious healthcare technology product that could be presented to:

* CEO
* Hospital/medical center management
* Medical director
* Operations director
* IT director

It must look expensive, trustworthy, sophisticated and highly usable.

Do NOT make it look like a generic AI startup.

Do NOT overuse futuristic neon effects.

Do NOT make it look like a template.

The visual language should communicate:

**Trust + Healthcare + Premium + Technology + Human Care**

Make responses feel conversational.
